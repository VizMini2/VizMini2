Node graph
==========

.. clip:: node_graph

Nodes and topics as a graph, like ``rqt_graph``. It refreshes every 2 seconds.

* Filter by name, and hide debug topics or topics nobody listens to (*dead ends*).
* Pinch to zoom, drag to move and double-tap to fit everything on screen.
* Tap a node or topic to highlight its connections; on the phone, tap a node again to open its
  parameters. Lifecycle nodes are marked *LC*.

Participants that don't announce ROS node names (other DDS programs) appear with their DDS name.
On the phone the graph is also available from Home, without creating a tab.

On the PC, zoom with the mouse wheel, drag to move and double-click to fit.
