Topics
======

.. clip:: topics

All the topics on the network, like ``ros2 topic list``, with a filter and how many publishers and
subscribers each one has.

Tap a topic to see:

* its **last message**, updated live (*Pause* to freeze it);
* its **rate** (``x Hz · N messages``), like ``ros2 topic hz``;
* the **publishers and subscribers**, node by node (on the phone, tap one to open its inspector).

The message can be shown for any message type bundled with the app (more than 300); for any other,
*Define the type…* lets you paste its definition (:ref:`your own types <custom-types>`). Inside a
:doc:`dashboard`, drag a topic from the list onto a widget or an empty spot to show it there (on the
phone, hold it first).

On the PC the topic opens inside the tab (the arrow at the top goes back to the list), and in a
dashboard you can also drag a numeric field of the message, by the handle next to it, onto a plot.
