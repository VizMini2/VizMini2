TF tree
=======

.. clip:: tf_tree

The robot's TF tree, like ``rqt_tf_tree``. It refreshes every 2 seconds.

* Static frames are drawn with a dashed border; each dynamic frame shows how long ago it was
  updated, in orange once it goes more than 5 seconds without updates.
* Tap a frame to see its parent and how long ago it was updated.
* Pinch to zoom, drag to move and double-tap to fit.

On the phone it is also available from Home, without creating a tab. On the PC, zoom with the mouse
wheel and double-click to fit.
