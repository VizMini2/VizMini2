Language
========

**Config → Language:** *System default*, *English* or *Español*. The app reloads its screens to
apply it. On Android 13 and newer you can also change it from the system settings, under the app's
language.

Tab types, the changelog and the catalog videos follow the language too. Next to it, **Theme**
chooses light, dark or the same as the system.

On the PC
---------

**Configuration → Appearance → Language:** *System*, *English* or *Español*; the window changes
right away. *System* follows the computer's language (``LANG`` on Linux, the language settings on Windows). The same card has **Theme** and
**Interface size** (*Compact*, *Normal* or *Large*).
