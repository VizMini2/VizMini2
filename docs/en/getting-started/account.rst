Your account (optional)
=======================

You don't need an account: the app opens straight on Home and everything works without one. An
account only serves to **sync your robot profiles** across your devices (:doc:`../config/profiles`).

Link an account
---------------

On **Home**, the **Account (optional)** card shows Google, Microsoft and GitHub. Tap **Link** next to
one and pick your account. The first time, your VizMini2 account is created with that account's name
and email; no password or email check needed. Your profiles start syncing right away.

If you already had an account with the same email, the app tells you: link the method you used the
first time and add the new one from Home.

Linking needs internet. After that you can use the app offline, for example connected only to the
robot's Wi-Fi: profiles sync when you are back online.

Sign-in methods
---------------

Once signed in, the card shows your email and your **Sign-in methods**. Tap **Link** next to Google,
Microsoft or GitHub to also sign in with it to the same account, with the same profiles. **Unlink**
removes it (you always keep at least one).

Accounts created with earlier versions using email and password keep working: they stay signed in
and appear as *Email and password* among the methods.

Sign out
--------

**Sign out** on the same card. You stay in the app: your tabs and profiles stay on this phone, but
they no longer sync with the cloud. To delete the account for good, see :doc:`../accounts/delete`.

On the PC
---------

**Configuration → Account (optional)**. **Continue with** Google, Microsoft or GitHub opens your
browser to sign in; then come back to the app. You can also sign in or create an account with an
email and password. Once signed in, the card shows your email, **Sync now** and **Sign out**. It
is the same account as on the phone, with the same profiles. Linking or unlinking sign-in methods
and deleting the account are done from the phone (:doc:`../accounts/delete`).
