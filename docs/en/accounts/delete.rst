Delete your account
===================

On **Home**, in the card with your email, tap **Delete my account** and sign in again with Google,
Microsoft or GitHub to confirm (accounts from earlier versions with email and password enter their
password instead).

This deletes, right away and for good:

* your account (you can't sign in with it any more);
* your name, email and linked sign-in methods;
* your robot profiles in the cloud.

You stay in the app and can keep using it without an account: tabs, profiles, recordings and
settings stored on the phone stay until you uninstall it.

The PC app has no **Delete my account** button: delete it from the phone, or by email as below.
Afterwards, **Sign out** on the PC (Configuration → Account (optional)).

<mailto:jc.manzanares.serrano@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20VizMini2%20account>`_ from your
account's address with the subject *Delete my VizMini2 account*. See also the
`privacy policy <https://ros-2-viz-mini.web.app/privacy>`_.
